AI-Accelerated Modernization & Sustenance

Tech Debt Is a Balance Sheet item. we Pay It Down.

Legacy isn't a label, it's a tax. We use an AI-trained modernization team to refactor, re-platform, and re-architect without the 18-month rewrite that never ships. And after the migration, we stay on to maintain what we built.

Let's Modernize

Our Modernization Framework

1.  Assess

Audit codebase, infra, and team. Quantify the debt in money, not feelings.

2. Roadmap

Sequence migrations by risk and ROI. No big-bang rewrites; every sprint ships value.

3. Migrate

Incrementally re-platform, refactor, and re-architect parallel to your feature roadmap.

4. Stabilize

Harden the new stack: observability, runbooks, load tests & SLOs before handoff.

5. Sustain

We don't leave. We maintain what we built and keep it current, secure, and fast.
